# Lazels Piece Finder

A single offline HTML file that identifies which stamp of the Lazels ships set a piece
belongs to: the **5¢ Royal William** (1933) or the **50¢ Bluenose** (1929).

- Open `Lazels-Piece-Finder.html` on your phone, for example from the Documents app.
- Take or choose a photo of one or more pieces, face-up, on a dark background.
- Each piece is labelled with its stamp and a confidence level. "Show where it goes"
  highlights the matching spot on the stamp.

Everything runs on the device; no internet connection is needed and nothing is uploaded.
The backs of the pieces are plain brushed metal and identical on both stamps, so matching
uses the printed front.

## Library

Every identified piece is saved to a library. Open it from the **Library** tab to see each
piece again, where it goes, and whether it's already in the puzzle. If a piece was put on the
wrong stamp or in the wrong spot, open it and pick the other stamp, or tap **Move it by hand**
to drag and turn it into the right place.

The web version saves the library privately to your Claude account. The offline file saves it
in the browser on the device you use it on.
